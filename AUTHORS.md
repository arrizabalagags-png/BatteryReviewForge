# Authors and collaboration / 作者与合作

**郭硕（Shuo Guo） · 姜金龙（Jinlong Jiang）**  
上海理工大学能源材料科学研究院  
Institute of Energy Materials Science, University of Shanghai for Science and Technology

BatteryReviewForge is a collaboration to make battery Review writing and scientific figure production easier to reuse, inspect and improve. The repository's [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) lists both collaborators for software citation. Specific code and documentation changes are traceable through the repository history; this page does not assign unverified individual roles or imply that every example figure is an experimental result from either collaborator.

我们希望把反复花在绘图、拼图和改稿上的精力还给科研问题本身。项目欢迎同行以真实问题、可核查的更正和原创工具参与建设。参与办法见 [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)。
