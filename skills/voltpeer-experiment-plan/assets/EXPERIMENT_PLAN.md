**Primary outcome and decision rule:** What raw measurement and comparison would support or weaken the stated claim? State uncertainty/replicates before interpreting differences.

**Secondary checks:** Which observation would distinguish the main explanation from an alternative? Identify when data are correlative only.

**Protocol record:** Electrode area/loading, electrolyte amount, N/P or Li excess if relevant, formation, SOC, temperature, pressure, current/capacity/voltage limits, rest duration and all deviations. Link to the laboratory's approved SOP and instrument method; do not paste unsafe generic settings.

**Raw-data capture:** Instrument exports, units, sign conventions, timestamps/cycle numbering, calibration or reference electrode, sample/batch IDs, failed runs and exclusions. Preserve raw files.

**Analysis / figures:** For each figure, list source file, axes and units, transformation or formula, required conditions, and what must **not** be inferred. Separate experimental observation from model or hypothesis.

**Unresolved before execution:** Unknown scientific settings, controls, replication, safety review, instrument capability and owner decision.
