# Battery terminology ledger starter

Use only entries that occur in the supplied draft. The author's material names, definitions and formulas take priority. Do not expand or rename an abbreviation by guessing. English alternatives in the table are choices for a manuscript, not permission to alternate names within it.

| Concept / 中文 | Canonical English form or useful expansion | Editorial boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 锂离子电池 | lithium-ion battery (LIB) | Do not replace with lithium-metal battery |
| 锂金属电池 | lithium-metal battery (LMB) | Preserve the actual negative-electrode inventory |
| 钠离子电池 | sodium-ion battery (SIB; NIB if established) | Lock the manuscript's abbreviation |
| 水系锌电池 | aqueous zinc battery; aqueous zinc-ion battery (AZIB) when defined | Do not infer an exclusive Zn²⁺ insertion mechanism from the technology name |
| 初始无负极 | initially anode-free | Describes the starting configuration; metal can form during charge |
| 全固态 | all-solid-state | Do not assign this label to a gel or a liquid-containing system without its definition |
| 准固态 | quasi-solid-state | Preserve the liquid/gel qualifier |
| 固态电解质 | solid electrolyte (SE) / solid-state electrolyte (SSE) | Follow the author's chosen abbreviation |
| 固体聚合物电解质 | solid polymer electrolyte (SPE) | Not interchangeable with gel polymer electrolyte |
| 凝胶聚合物电解质 | gel polymer electrolyte (GPE) | Keep solvent content/composition when supplied |
| 复合电解质 | composite electrolyte | Preserve the specified matrix and filler |
| 正极 / 负极 | positive electrode / negative electrode | Stable physical labels across charging and discharging |
| 工作 / 对 / 参比电极 | working / counter / reference electrode | Keep their experimental roles separate |
| 集流体 | current collector | Not the active material or a complete electrode |
| 硬碳 | hard carbon (HC) | Not a synonym for graphite or all disordered carbons |
| 石墨 | graphite | Keep lithiation and Li plating conceptually separate |
| 活性物质 | active material | Preserve whether a normalization uses active or composite mass |
| 高镍层状氧化物 | Ni-rich layered oxide | Preserve composition, NCM/NMC ordering and sample labels |
| 普鲁士蓝类似物 | Prussian blue analogue (PBA) | Use analogue/analog consistently with the target style |
| NASICON | sodium superionic conductor (NASICON) | Confirm whether it denotes a structural family or a specific composition |
| 硫化聚丙烯腈 | sulfurized polyacrylonitrile (SPAN) | Not equivalent to a conventional sulfur-carbon composite |
| 电极/电解质界面 | electrode/electrolyte interface | A boundary; not automatically a reaction-product interphase |
| 固体电解质界相 | solid-electrolyte interphase (SEI) | Confirm the author's expansion; avoid silently substituting interface |
| 正极电解质界相 | cathode-electrolyte interphase (CEI) | Keep its electrode assignment explicit |
| 电双层 | electric double layer (EDL) | Not a synonym for SEI or an applied coating |
| 原位生成界相 | in situ formed interphase | Different from an ex situ deposited artificial layer |
| 有机/无机富集 | organic-rich / inorganic-rich | Composition and sampling boundary need evidence |
| 溶剂化 | solvation | Not adsorption, desolvation or migration |
| 第一溶剂化壳层 | first solvation shell | A coordination definition/cutoff may be model dependent |
| 配位数 | coordination number (CN) | Dimensionless; preserve species pair and integration cutoff |
| 配位强度 | coordination strength | Do not equate with measured conductivity |
| 脱溶剂化 | desolvation | Distinct from diffusion through an interphase |
| 溶剂分离离子对 | solvent-separated ion pair (SSIP) | Preserve the author's classification rules |
| 接触离子对 | contact ion pair (CIP) | Different from an aggregate |
| 离子聚集体 | aggregate (AGG) | AGG population alone does not prove better cell performance |
| 阴离子富集溶剂化 | anion-rich solvation | Distinct from anion-derived interphase composition |
| 弱溶剂化电解液 | weakly solvating electrolyte (WSE) | Not necessarily a concentrated electrolyte |
| 高浓电解液 | high-concentration electrolyte (HCE) | Retain its concentration definition |
| 局域高浓电解液 | localized high-concentration electrolyte (LHCE) | Identify salt, solvent and diluent separately |
| 稀释剂 / 共溶剂 | diluent / cosolvent | Their coordination and chemical functions can differ |
| 水活度 | water activity | A thermodynamic quantity, not simply the amount of free water |
| 自由水 | free water | Preserve the operational/spectroscopic/model definition |
| 沉积/剥离 | plating/stripping | Preserve which metal, electrode and half-cycle direction |
| 嵌入/脱出 | insertion/extraction; intercalation/deintercalation when structural meaning is established | Do not turn a conversion reaction into intercalation |
| 合金化/脱合金化 | alloying/dealloying | Distinct from adsorption and deposition |
| 转化反应 | conversion reaction | Preserve reactants/products and electron count |
| 溶解/沉积 | dissolution/deposition | Can coexist with insertion in aqueous cathodes |
| 成核过电位 | nucleation overpotential | Not the entire charge-discharge voltage gap |
| 极化 | polarization | Identify the reported definition rather than guessing a component |
| 析氢 | hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) | A side reaction; reduced HER does not prove zero corrosion |
| 穿梭 | shuttle / shuttling | Identify polysulfide, polyiodide or other species |
| 死锂 | electrochemically inactive lithium; dead lithium if established | Distinguish electrically isolated metal and Li in reaction products where needed |
| 库仑效率 | Coulombic efficiency (CE) | Protocol-specific charge ratio; not energy efficiency |
| 初始库仑效率 | initial Coulombic efficiency (ICE) | Preserve formation protocol and insertion/extraction convention |
| 容量保持率 | capacity retention | Reference capacity and cycle range are part of the metric |
| 能量保持率 | energy retention | Not capacity retention |
| 比容量 | specific capacity | Usually mass normalized; name the mass basis |
| 面容量 | areal capacity | Area normalized; not areal current |
| 比能量 | specific energy | Mass normalized; retain cell/stack/active-mass boundary |
| 体积能量密度 | volumetric energy density | Retain cell/stack/electrolyte-volume boundary |
| 倍率性能 | rate capability | Identify charging versus discharging performance |
| 快充 | fast charging | State SOC endpoints, time/rate and conditions when supplied |
| 日历寿命 | calendar life | Rest/storage time; not cycling life |
| 循环寿命 | cycle life | Test endpoint, cycle definition and retention threshold matter |
| 自放电 | self-discharge | Different from charge-cycle capacity fade |
| 电荷转移电阻 | charge-transfer resistance (Rct) | A fitted/assigned process; not bulk diffusion coefficient |
| 交换电流密度 | exchange current density | Preserve method and normalization |
| 离子迁移数 | ion transference number | Use the stated ion and method; distinguish apparent and model values |
| 负正极容量比 | negative-to-positive capacity ratio (N/P) | Capacity ratio, not mass ratio |
| 电解液/硫比 | electrolyte-to-sulfur ratio (E/S) | Usually volume/mass; preserve actual units |
| 电解液/容量比 | electrolyte-to-capacity ratio (E/C) | Often mass/capacity; confirm definition |
| 原位 | in situ | Performed in place; does not necessarily mean during operation |
| 工况 / 运行中 | operando | Observation during operation linked to performance; do not upgrade ex situ to operando |
| 非原位 | ex situ | Preserve sampling, transfer and post-cycling boundaries |
| 密度泛函理论 | density functional theory (DFT) | Calculation supports a model-dependent inference |
| 分子动力学 | molecular dynamics (MD) | Preserve force field, cutoff and state conditions when relevant |
| 径向分布函数 | radial distribution function (RDF) | Not itself a measured coordination number |
| 电化学阻抗谱 | electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) | Preserve circuit/assignment uncertainty |
| 恒流充放电 | galvanostatic charge-discharge (GCD) | Current controlled; retain asymmetric rates |
| 循环伏安 | cyclic voltammetry (CV) | Scan rate and peak assignments are not automatically kinetic constants |
| 线性扫描伏安 | linear sweep voltammetry (LSV) | A measured stability onset is not a universal cell voltage rating |
| XPS | X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy | Surface/depth and fitting context bound composition claims |
| ToF-SIMS | time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectrometry | Fragment assignment is not direct intact-molecule quantification |
| 液流电池 | redox flow battery (RFB) | Preserve membrane, tank/electrolyte and pumping boundaries |
| 有机水系液流 | aqueous organic redox flow battery (AORFB) | Different from every aqueous metal battery |
| 负/正电解液 | negolyte / posolyte | Anolyte/catholyte are alternatives if defined; do not vary them for style |
| 活性物渗透 | active-species crossover | Distinct from chemical decomposition |
| 电压效率 | voltage efficiency (VE) | Does not equal CE; product conventions depend on definitions |
| 能量效率 | energy efficiency (EE) | Retain cell-level or system-level measurement boundary |
| 往返效率 | round-trip efficiency (RTE) | Identify whether pumping and auxiliary consumption are included |
| 容量利用率 | capacity utilization | Delivered fraction of a specified theoretical or available capacity |
| 多电子储能 | multi-electron storage | Molecular electron count and accessible cell capacity are distinct |
