# Journal selection and submission preflight

Use this when the user is selecting a journal, preparing a proposal, cover letter, or submission package, or asking whether a Review is ready to submit. Journal rules are time-sensitive. Check the official journal and publisher pages for the *exact article type* and record each URL and access date in a journal ledger. Use [JOURNAL_CHECK.md](assets/templates/JOURNAL_CHECK.md) if there is no existing checklist.

## Choose the target using the manuscript

Compare audience, article type, scope, expected level of critical synthesis, invitation/proposal rules, length, display-item limits, reference style, open-access policy and cost, and recent comparable Reviews. Identify the manuscript changes required for each plausible venue. A prestige ranking by itself is not a fit analysis. If a proposal or editor inquiry is required or prudent, prepare a concrete synopsis and evidence of differentiation first.

## Official-rule ledger

For each requirement record: article type, requirement, status (`checked`, `not applicable`, `unresolved`), official URL, access date, exact supporting passage or section, file/location checked, and owner of any open action. Re-check close to submission; instructions for another article type or an older cached page do not settle a conflict. If two sources disagree, record both and escalate the unresolved interpretation to the corresponding author or journal rather than declaring a pass.

Typical items include title/abstract/keywords, word and reference limits, figure/table count and specifications, graphical abstract or highlights, broader-context or significance text, review proposal form, data/code availability, competing interests, funding, author contributions, ethics and AI-use declarations where required, cover letter, permissions, supplementary material, and file naming. Check author names/order and affiliations with the authors; a task assignment is not an authorship decision.

## Package gate

Check that the cover letter names the actual journal and article type, the manuscript and supplemental files are current and internally consistent, figure citations and captions match files, references resolve, permissions are documented, and the submission portal's requested statements are present. For reused artwork, a correct credit line does not replace a permission grant or compatible license. Check *effective* image resolution at final print size.

Separate `ready`, `ready with named author decision`, and `blocked` items. A package is ready only when every applicable requirement is checked and the authors have approved the science and authorship. If the user has authorized submission, use the approved files; otherwise deliver the reviewable package and ledger. Do not send editor inquiries or submit a manuscript on the strength of a checklist alone.
