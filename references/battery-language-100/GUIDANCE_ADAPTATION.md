# Guidance consulted and independently adapted

This is skill engineering for generic battery manuscript polishing. It is not a polish of an actual author manuscript and does not claim compliance with any journal's current submission rules.

The locally installed `nature-polishing` manifest reported version **6.6.0**. The engineering task consulted its routing file, manifest and all declared core files, followed only by the relevant research/review, English, generic-journal, Results and Discussion fragments. The main-text and Discussion shared guidance were also read. Unrelated journal-specific, layout and book/phrasebank references were not copied or loaded wholesale.

| Guidance path, relative to the installed skills root | Rule adopted | VoltPeer adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| `nature-polishing/SKILL.md`, `manifest.yaml` | Detect paper type, section, language and journal; load only relevant guidance | Route from supplied text with generic defaults; routine choices do not become approval gates |
| `nature-shared/core/reader-workflow.md` | Orient the reader to relevance, contribution, evidence, reuse and scope | Express concrete battery questions and test conditions without copying the five-question presentation |
| `nature-shared/core/paper-type-taxonomy.md` | Distinguish research and synthesis work | Research and review edits use different section jobs; the public guide remains usable for other manuscript types |
| `nature-shared/core/ethics.md` | Preserve attribution and avoid invented data/references | Preserve citation keys and evidence boundaries; verify a current journal policy only when it is relevant |
| `nature-shared/core/terminology-ledger.md` | Establish canonical terms before editing | Add electrode roles, cell pairs, chemistry formulas, normalization bases and protocol-specific metric definitions |
| `nature-polishing/static/core/stance.md` | Language serves the supplied argument; avoid invented novelty/mechanisms | Expose gaps rather than inventing an improved scientific argument |
| `nature-polishing/static/core/failure-modes.md` | Diagnose structural and evidential problems before sentence polish | Check section function, metric boundary and claim strength before improving rhythm |
| `nature-polishing/static/core/output-format.md` | Return polished prose and concise material revision notes | Keep output proportional; substantial compression gets an auditable allocation/word-count note |
| `nature-polishing/static/fragments/paper_type/research.md` | Make the question, observation and implication coherent | Preserve the author's question and test design while clarifying the evidence chain |
| `nature-polishing/static/fragments/paper_type/review.md` | Synthesize literature by argument and expose disagreement | Preserve comparability conditions and avoid manufacturing a pooled performance ranking |
| `nature-polishing/static/fragments/section/results.md` | Results establish claims with visible conditions and quantitative support | Add battery-specific quantity, cell and protocol tuples; keep contradictory regimes visible |
| `nature-polishing/static/fragments/section/discussion.md` | Discussion interprets findings and names boundaries | Preserve alternate mechanisms, cell-configuration limitations and unresolved practical conditions |
| `nature-polishing/static/fragments/language/en.md` | Clear subjects, one controlling idea and sentence economy | The 10–30-word range is a readability heuristic, not a hard limit that could erase necessary conditions |
| `nature-polishing/static/fragments/journal/generic.md` | Use generic defaults when a journal is not named | Do not imply flagship Nature rules or official Nature policy |
| `nature-shared/core/main-text-discipline.md` | Keep the shortest sufficient evidence chain; avoid revision accretion | Remove duplicated claims while retaining conclusion-changing battery failures and reporting facts |
| `nature-shared/core/discussion-argument-language.md` | Calibrate observation, interpretation, extrapolation and limitations | Apply a battery evidence ladder from measurements to assignments to causal tests and application claims |

The public domain guide, glossary, function patterns, corpus observations and evaluation examples are newly written and self-contained. They are not a redistribution of the consulted skill, its fragments or article paragraphs. Routine scientific terms are used in their ordinary technical sense. The 100 article records document the actual author texts inspected; inclusion is separate from endorsing the article's prose, data or conclusions.

The metadata checks verify DOI identity, journal-article type and normalized title agreement. They do not verify every mechanism or establish a performance meta-analysis. The evaluation checker detects specified factual and scope risks conservatively. Its self-test demonstrates those checks on synthetic outputs and makes no claim about unrun assistant/model behavior.
